Add the `Checkbox` form primitive (Epic 2 Inputs).

- `Checkbox` — wraps `@radix-ui/react-checkbox`; token-only with
  `checked`/`indeterminate`/`disabled` and `aria-invalid` states. The indicator
  shows a `Check` icon when checked and swaps to a `Minus` icon in the
  indeterminate state. Ships a matching `CheckboxSkeleton` (ADR-0005).

Exported from `@gambitech/ds`. No hardcoded colors; light/dark ready.
